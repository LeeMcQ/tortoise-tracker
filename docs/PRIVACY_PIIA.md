# POPIA / Personal Information Impact Assessment Working Template

**Status:** Must be completed and signed by the responsible party / Information Officer before production.

## Processing activity
Public and staff collection of wildlife observations, photographs, timestamps, device-derived location and staff research/health records.

## Data minimisation already implemented
- No public visitor account required.
- No visitor name/email/phone required in the standard public workflow.
- Product analytics reject coordinates, names, email addresses and observation notes.
- Public APIs do not expose exact wildlife coordinates.
- Restricted health/research tables require authenticated roles.

## Decisions still required
- Final responsible party and Information Officer contact details.
- Lawful basis and purpose statement for each processing activity.
- Retention periods for raw observations, photos, staff records, audit logs and product telemetry.
- Cross-border/cloud processing assessment.
- Data-subject request procedure where personal information exists.
- Security-compromise notification workflow and ownership.
- Final privacy notice wording and records of processing activities.
- Whether any photograph containing a person is rejected, redacted or retained under a documented exception.

## Photo policy proposed
Client re-encoding strips normal EXIF metadata before upload; database GPS is stored separately. Users are instructed to avoid identifiable people.
