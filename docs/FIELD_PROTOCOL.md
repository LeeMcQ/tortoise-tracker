# Field Observation Protocol

## Public observer
1. Do not handle, feed, chase or reposition the tortoise.
2. Scan QR or enter visible ID; if no identifier exists use **Report untagged tortoise**.
3. Read the location explanation, then allow high-accuracy GPS.
4. Retry if accuracy exceeds the configured field threshold.
5. Photograph from a respectful distance. A clear dorsal shell image is preferred; for untagged animals, a lateral image is also useful.
6. Select behaviour, apparent condition and identification confidence.
7. Do not include personal information in notes or identifiable people in photographs.
8. Submit. If offline, confirm that the sighting is shown as pending sync.

## Staff measurement provenance
Every measurement must retain animal, date/time, measurement type, value, unit, method, confidence and recorder. Instrument/calibration identity should be added where relevant.

## Physical identification/device gate
No QR/PIT/RFID/GNSS/LoRaWAN or other device attachment method may be operationally adopted on the basis of this software design alone. Obtain appropriate wildlife/veterinary and regulatory approval first.
